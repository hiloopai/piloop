---
title: Tracks
description: Several objectives for one piece of code, on one trunk.
---

A **track** is one objective of a campaign that has several, such as the accuracy of a model and its cost to run.
Tracks are experimental. `piloop init --example titanic-tracks` makes a campaign with two tracks:

```toml
[[metrics]]
name = "accuracy"
direction = "higher"
role = "primary"
track = "accuracy"
margin = 0.02          # what a cost keep may lose
floor = 0.74           # and never below this

[[metrics]]
name = "cost_seconds"
direction = "lower"
role = "primary"
track = "cost"
margin = 0.3           # what an accuracy keep may add
floor = 2.0            # and never past this
repeats = 3

[tracks.accuracy]
share = 0.75           # of the implementer slots
addendum = "briefs/accuracy.md"

[tracks.cost]
share = 0.25
addendum = "briefs/cost.md"
```

`piloop status titanic-tracks` shows one line for each track:

```text
## Tracks

- accuracy: accuracy 0.7416 at the head (+0 on the start); floor 0.74, 0.0016 to spare; 0 trunk keeps; share 0.75 of 4 slots, 0 running; 0 runnable ideas.
- cost: cost_seconds 1.66 at the head (+0 on the start); floor 2, 0.34 to spare; 0 trunk keeps; share 0.25 of 4 slots, 0 running; 0 runnable ideas.
```

## The rules

- Each idea names its track: `piloop idea add titanic-tracks "Add a family-size feature" --track accuracy`.
- A candidate is judged on every track whose primary it has a value for: first its idea's track, then the others.
  On each track, the other tracks' primaries are its guardrails, each with its own `margin` and `floor`.
- The candidate gets the best of these verdicts, and `candidates.track` names its track. A tie goes to its idea's
  track. Each track's verdict is a `candidate.judged` event.
- All tracks share one head. A keep on any track moves it.
- A free slot goes to the track that is furthest below its `share`. The shares sum to 1. A share of 0 stops a track.
- `addendum` is a file that piloop adds to the brief of the track's runs.

A campaign with one primary has one track, and needs no `[tracks]` table.
