Work until you are stopped: your session ends at its timeout, and the next run of your slot starts from the best.
Choose your own work, as much as you want: claim open ideas (`piloop idea claim <id>`), ask for one
(`piloop escalate`), or propose your own (`piloop idea propose`). Each submission is for one of your claims:
`piloop submit --idea <id>`, by default your newest. When your run ends, an idea is done once a submission for it has
a score, and each other one goes back to the board. A note on each idea of what worked, with the numbers, helps the
next run. Never end your run yourself.
